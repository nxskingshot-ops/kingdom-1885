ⓘ How is the score calculated?

Internal NXS model: 15 points per recorded castle upgrade, +10 for castle and power in the same interval, 1.5 per power alert, plus 1 point per 10M retained-member growth. The cumulative score adds the recorded interval scores once each. Repeated power alerts across different intervals remain separate observations.

The first historical periods are aggregated and their lengths differ. This activity index is not a percentage, winning probability, or the official Kingshot matchmaking formula.
