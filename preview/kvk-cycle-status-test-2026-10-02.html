Prep Mechanics & Rewards

Useful rules to surface in the KvK Prep dashboard · Battle Phase and King's Castle Battle are tracked separately
Official + reference data
🏆 Preparation result

The kingdom with more total Preparation points wins the phase. The Prep winner attacks during Battle; the Prep loser defends.

🎯 Duel Group bonus

A Kingdom Preparation Point Bonus earned by finishing 1st in a daily Duel Group becomes active in the next phase/day, not retroactively.

🎁 Daily rewards

Three personal reward chests are available each Prep day. Reference data says the final chest usually requires 200,000 points.

🏅 State Medals

Reference data describes one State Medal per Prep day (5 total), with the sixth earned by participating in the Battle Phase.

⚔ Battle Phase

Battle Phase starts Saturday 10 Oct at 10:00 UTC and runs to 22:00 UTC. This is the overall PvP phase window, not the same thing as the King's Castle fight.

🏰 King's Castle Battle

The castle fight is a separate battle objective inside the Battle Phase. Current official guidance describes a 5-hour castle contest; victory goes to the kingdom with the longest total occupation, or by holding it continuously for 2.5 hours.
