## V3 evaluation criteria
1. Evidence grading per conclusion: VERIFIED / INDICATIVE / UNKNOWN; record source, observation timestamp, sample size, and comparability.
2. Separate raw power, kingdom rank, category balance, alliance placement, and combat-role suitability.
3. Role suggestions require explicitly named missing battle reports, buffs, formation and troop data; no automatic certified role.
4. Avoid misleading score precision, false green verdicts, and top-15 guarantees on partial samples.
5. Compare V2 and V3 side by side on identical anonymized or authorized live-read-only fixtures, including missing and stale inputs.
6. Keep all conclusions in English in the eventual app.
