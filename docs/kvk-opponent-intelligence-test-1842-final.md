## Executive summary
The test confirms that a fresh observer account plus public web data is enough to build a useful opponent profile without grinding the account to TC19.

Kingdom #1842 shows a clear top-end outlier in **Lord Z [FFS]**, but the kingdom is not weak behind him. The Mystic Trial curve remains comparatively broad through at least rank 17.

This is exactly the type of opponent profile the future KvK Head-to-Head view should identify: **mega-whale impact plus kingdom depth**, not one or the other.

## What we learned about data collection
A new observer character needed roughly **40 minutes** to unlock the rankings needed for useful scouting.

Crucially, the observer could already view the **Mystic Trial leaderboard before TC19**. TC19 is therefore not worth grinding solely for opponent scouting.

### Minimum future scouting pack
Only these six ranking screenshots should be treated as the default:
1. Alliance Power
2. Personal Power
3. Mystic Trial Top ~20
4. Hero Power
5. Total Companion Power
6. Town Center / Truegold

Public web data should be collected first. The observer account then verifies or fills the gaps.

## What still remains unknown
The following should only be collected for a handful of high-impact players if tactically necessary:
- individual Governor Gear
- individual Governor Charms
- individual Research
- verified primary rally-lead / garrison role
- formation-specific battle buffs and combat stats

The Nexus App should show these as **missing**, not estimate them.

## Recommended Nexus App logic
True Power Intelligence should split opponent analysis into:
- **Mega-Whale Impact**
- **Top-player Depth**
- **Alliance Distribution**
- **TG Depth**
- **Hero / Companion signals**
- **Data Coverage**

No fixed danger threshold should be hard-coded yet. The app should display measured gaps and let historical KvK cases teach us which differences matter in practice.

## Test conclusion
The workflow is viable and much more efficient than manually collecting full profiles.

**Public data → observer ranking pack → identify 3–10 important players → targeted screenshots only if needed.**

For #1842, Lord Z would already trigger a strong Mega-Whale Watch because he is simultaneously #1 in Personal Power, Mystic Trial, Hero Power and Companion Power, while the rest of the kingdom still has meaningful depth behind him.
